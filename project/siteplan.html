Website Plan

WDD 131 - Dynamic Web Fundamentals

Site Name

[QuestForge Tales]

Why this name was chosen: I chose this name because it highlights the core mechanic of the site: crafting and embarking on text-based journeys where the player's choices forge the outcome. It sets an adventurous, imaginative tone for users looking to dive into an immersive story.

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to provide an immersive, text-based interactive fiction game where users can read a branching narrative and make critical choices that alter the plot. The site aims to deliver an entertaining, nostalgic, and seamless gameplay experience directly in the browser without requiring external downloads.

Target Audience & Scenarios

Think about the people visiting your site. Below are at least two strategic questions a typical visitor might expect answered when browsing this website:

  • Scenario 1: I only have 10 minutes during my lunch break. Can I easily start playing the game, and will it save my progress or let me restart quickly if I hit a Game Over?
  • Scenario 2: As a fan of classic tabletop RPGs, does this website offer meaningful choice mechanics (like tracking my character's inventory or health) that impact the story, or is it just a linear path?

Usage Plan: The Primary color is used for headings. The Secondary color serves as a background contrast block. The Accent color highlights key borders and focus points.

Typography

The text layout relies on the following fonts to build structure, hierarchy, and strong readability:

  • Heading Font: Georgia - Used for all `h1`, `h2`, and `h3` titles to draw emphasis.
  • Body Font: Merriweather - Used for all paragraphs, lists, and main readable structural text blocks.
